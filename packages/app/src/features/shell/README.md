# shell

The unlocked app's frame. Desktop: 64px rail (Accounts, Backups, Bots soon, Lock, Settings) and a 60px topbar. Phone: header with title, status and actions, then a floating tab bar, the dock (Accounts, Backups, Bots soon, Settings). The stage behind it is `.bg-stage` in `App.vue`.

- `shellRoute` is the layout route; `router.ts` nests every screen under it as children.
- Pages are layers (`ShellPages`, `useShellLayers`, `ShellLayer`), each with its own scroller and `providePage` context (`core/page.ts`): the layer reads the scroller's offset once per scroll, in the capture phase, for every `useScrollTop()` reader. A section root with `meta.resident` mounts on its first visit and stays until the shell unmounts (a lock leaves the shell route): hidden with `content-visibility: hidden` and `inert`, it keeps its scroll, rows and filters. Any other page lives while current and starts at the top. A route's `header` named view (the Accounts filters) renders above the layer's scroller: it travels with its page, and nothing scrolls under its blur. A switch plays CSS keyframes on the two layers and settles when their animations finish.
- A screen feeds the topbar through `PageTopbar` (`#components/PageTopbar.vue`), which teleports its parts into the shell's outlets (`core/topbarOutlets.ts`) while its page is current; otherwise they wait mounted in a hidden holder, so a switch moves them instead of mounting them again. The route's `meta.nav` picks the highlighted entry, `meta.tabBar: false` hides the phone tab bar for a drill-down.
- `meta.hero` marks a detail page its list item morphs into on desktop (`:id` names the item): `useRouteMotion` runs the swap inside `document.startViewTransition`, sets `data-route-motion="hero-open|hero-close"` on `<html>` and the item's id through `setRouteHero` (`core/routeHero.ts`), and waits two ticks before the second capture (frames are frozen until then, so a list that measures through a ResizeObserver cannot lay out; a card it has not rendered gets a fade instead of a morph). The CSS in `ShellPages` names the `data-vt-hero` elements `account-hero` only while that attribute is set. Only on Gamify: phones drill and Simple shifts; motion off and browsers without View Transitions swap at once.
- `SyncStatus` from `#features/backups` is the Drive sync indicator pages place in the `status` slot.
- A section's rail and dock entry reopens the page the section was left on (the profile it showed, without the overlays in its query); pressed inside its section, it goes to the section's root. `useSectionMemory` records it while the shell lives, so a lock starts over. A lock also drops every toast (`useLockClearsToasts`): an Undo or a copied secret belongs to the open vault.
- The rail and the dock each glide one highlight to the active entry (`translate` from an index var). The rail's bottom Settings button keeps its own fill: it is bottom-anchored, so gliding there would need layout reads.
- The dock floats over the phone scrollers (`layer-dock`; each `ShellLayer` pads `--dock-clear` unless its route hides the tab bar). `useDock` follows the current page's `useScrollTop()`: it hides the dock on scroll down and shows it on scroll up, near the top and on navigation, with `services/dockScroll.ts` deciding. `ShellTabBar.vue` also sets `--dock-*` and `--toast-offset-bottom` on `:root` so phone toasts clear it.
- Settings opens through `useSettingsEntry()` from `#features/settings`: a window on desktop, the `/settings` screen on phones.

The rail's Lock button calls `useVaultSession().lock` from `#features/vault`. Unwired intents: Bots (planned, disabled).
